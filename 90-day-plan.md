## 🧭 The Core Engineering Formula
This is **NOT a theory project**. Every single day answers:
1. **WHAT AM I BUILDING TODAY?** (Concrete Project Name)
2. **WHAT AM I EXECUTING TODAY?** (Deterministic CLI / Code)
3. **WHAT CAN BREAK?** (Failure Challenge & Anti-Pattern)
4. **HOW WILL I DEBUG IT?** (Trace, Trap, Fix)
5. **WHAT EVIDENCE WILL I CAPTURE?** (Terminal Logs, JSON, Exit 0)
6. **WHAT WILL I POST TODAY?** (LinkedIn Post + 5 High-Res Technical Images)
